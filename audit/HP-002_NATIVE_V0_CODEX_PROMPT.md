## THE RULESET (CANON unless tagged)
**Structure.** Four periods of 600 s. A First Hunt opens the match, every period and every overtime period; each team picks any field player as its contestant. A false start is retaken. If tied after period 4, the first overtime is half length (300 s), runs the whole period, and uses the regulation-overtime rules. Later overtimes are sudden death; any score (full goal, half goal, or a penalty goal if ever introduced) ends it. Scrimmages follow tackles and scores. Breaks between periods and timeouts are deferred (not ruled). The clock stops on every dead ball.
**Ball.** No kicking: the ball is thrown or carried. Each player may dribble once per possession (throw ahead of himself and replay it); the allowance resets on any stoppage and when another player touches the ball. A second dribble is an offence minor foul: tackle count +1, scrimmage at the foul spot. There is a bounce option: a throw to a spot on the surface, then ball physics (skip pass, angle shot).
**Play clock.** 30 s. It carries through a tackle and is fresh (30 s, zero tackles) after a score or a turnover. Expiry is a turnover: the other team scrimmages at the spot or at its own 40 m line, whichever is closer to the goal it attacks. The clock runs during the snap window only if more than 0:10 remained at the tackle.
**Geometry.** Playing surface 100 x 100 m; touching on or beyond a boundary line is out of bounds at once (airborne players stay in bounds until a body part touches out). Goal plane at 0 m. Goal front view: semicircle of radius 3 m (6 m wide, 3 m high). Side view: quadrant of radius 3 m (3 m deep). A goal counts only if the whole ball crosses the goal plane inside the semicircle. Keeper Zone: field area closer than R from the goal centre, R = 20 m configured, arc itself outside, same for both teams; athletes judged on body centre, ball on ground position only (airborne ball over the zone counts as inside). One Archer platform per goal, centred on the goal plane, 5 m high, 5 m back from the boundary / goal plane, facing the opposing goal, static x,y, no blocked vision. (Replaces the old 15 m working default. Check the engine and arrow ballistics against this.)
**Scoring (flat until the self-play experiment reports).** Any goal 2 points; half goal 1 point. A carried-in goal is tracked as a Carry; for every other goal track release distance and success rate. A Keeper cannot score for his team; a ball he puts in his own goal counts for the opponents. Track per team: goals, distance progressed, turnovers, minor and major fouls, half-goal awards.
**Tackle count and Contact Scrimmage.** First legal grounding tackle: the offence keeps the ball; a Contact Scrimmage replaces the live recovery (1 m disc, 3 s). The line of scrimmage is the tackle spot, also inside the Keeper Zone. A first tackle with x less than 10 m from the goal plane resets the line to 10 m (first tackles only). The tackled carrier is the snapper; the tackler aligns opposite him or 5 m behind. Snap window 10 s after the referee sets the ball; missing it is count +1 and the spot moves 5 m back (at count 1 that is a turnover). Offside is judged against the scrimmage line (defined by the ball): offence offside is count +1 and the spot moves 5 m back; defence offside is count -1 (floor 0) and the spot moves 5 m closer to the goal attacked; offside is a procedural penalty, not a minor foul (no half goal at count 0). Second legal grounding tackle is a turnover: the other team scrimmages at the tackle spot or its own 40 m line, whichever is closer to the goal it attacks. After a score: 40 m scrimmage at the centre of the conceding team's own 40 m line, count 0, fresh 30 s, everyone at or behind their own 40 m line.
**Fouls and discipline (counts are per player).** Offence minor: count +1 (a minor at count 1 is a turnover). Defence minor: count -1 (floor 0); at count 0 the offence keeps the ball and is awarded half a goal in periods 1 to 3 and a full goal in period 4 and all overtime; restart by scrimmage at the ball or foul spot, whichever is closer to the goal attacked, count 0. Unintentional head contact is a minor. Majors: intentional head contact; intentional tripping of an off-ball player ("intentional" = a contact the athlete's own decision chose). First major: 120 s game-time exclusion with a substitute; second: disqualification. Fourth minor: 60 s exclusion; fifth: disqualification. A team always has 12 on the field (a substitute covers every exclusion or DQ); forfeit only if fewer than 12 of its 22 are available. An excluded player may return after his time.
**Contact.** Marking distance 5 m. Off-ball contact is legal below the neck and above the hip (shoulders, arms); hip jockeying, push and stab are legal; pulling and gripping are not (minor foul). A defender may wrap and grip the carrier from below the neck down; knee and joint contact on the carrier is legal.
**Keeper Zone rules.** Attackers: 5 s residence, expiry is a turnover. Keepers may stay indefinitely. Defenders may stay indefinitely while within 5 m of an offensive player. Keeper immunity, only inside his own zone: (a) no opponent-initiated contact while his team is on offence (has passive possession, including while the ball is loose or in the air); (b) no freeze in any phase. It depends on position at the instant of contact. Archers never target Keepers or anyone inside a Keeper Zone. ZR-1 (ball in own zone, own team has passive possession, 5 s with no reset), ZR-C (carrier crosses from outside into his own zone holding team control), ZR-T (deliberate pass, bounce pass included, enters own zone from outside and a teammate touches it first) each give RST-1: possession to the opponent, origin outside, zone clock 0 and stopped.
**Archers.** 25 arrows and at most 5 freezes per possession; freeze 5 s, post-freeze immunity 10 s, recovery protection 3 m / 2 s. An Archer may target all opposing field players while that team is on offence and in possession. Ammunition is spent at release; an illegal arrow is nullified at release but spends ammunition and draws a minor foul; a draw can be aborted (no ammunition spent). Archers cannot draw or fire until the Hunt ball has left the centre circle.
**Possession model (D1 R-01..R-22, ruleset 1.0.2).** Direct possession after 500 ms of uncontested control (tested at 250/500/1000 ms); contested = opposing players simultaneously touching the ball; passive possession = last team with direct possession, flips only after 500 ms of the other team's uncontested control; contest lapses direct possession and does not change passive possession; body contact alone does not contest; zone clock resets on ball leaving the zone, possession change, defender touch, restart, dead ball, timeout, period break. Existing `b1/possession.py` implements the table; wire it into the match loop so it is enforced, not just queried.
**Exact ties.** Shot release at the instant of clock expiry counts (only shots keep the in-flight exception); a coincident goal and catch is a goal; a coincident boundary touch and secure hold is out.
**Boundaries and restarts.** Out of play: possession unchanged until the restart awards it, then to the awarded team at once. Toss-in (ratified): from the crossing point (slid to the edge of a Keeper Zone on a goal line); thrower is a field player with feet outside the line; opponents at least 3 m away; ready at 1.5 s, throw within 4 s of ready; a released toss cannot score until another player controls it; the thrower cannot recover it.
**Deferred, do not build:** breaks and timeouts, Hunt penalties beyond the retake, fatigue and injury, rating development, any adaptive learning, M2 plan/message content (plumbing only, null mode), standard (non-flat) scoring.
**Architecture rules that stand.** Pipeline ObservationFrame -> AthleteView -> BeliefState -> ActionIntent -> CommandIngress -> MotorCommand -> PhysicalExecutionPort -> PhysicalOutcome. Athletes own their decisions: no externally prescribed formations or tactical assignments (formation-free), no hidden-attribute access, no rating development, uniform 0.70 ratings for the baseline run. Engine keeps true state for physics. Legacy PRISM overlay layers (PRISM_FIELD, PRISM_MANEUVERS, PRISM_OFFACT, prism_keeper_v0) stay off; the athlete decision must come from `decide()` in the B1 path. Kicking must not exist anywhere in the path.
