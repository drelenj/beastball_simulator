## Step 2 (done): foul ladder substitutes, forfeit rule, archer limits
- Always 12 on the field: after the engine's own refill, any team with fewer than 10 active field players (plus keeper and archer) is refilled at once from the bench (not injured, not DQ, not serving an exclusion). An excluded player returns to the bench when his time ends and is eligible as a substitute.
- Forfeit: the old 'minimum_lineup' ground (more than 3 players out) is replaced by: forfeit only if fewer than 12 of the team's 22 are available (not injured, not disqualified).
- Archers: every possession change gives both quivers 25 arrows (spent 0, 5 loaded) and resets the freeze count; at most 5 freezes per possession per shooting team; the sixth is refused ('freeze_limit' event).  Freeze 5 s + 10 s immunity is the engine's existing 15 s window (unchanged).  Reading: 'per possession' = each change of possession [R].
- Tests (scratch t5): minors 1-3 free, 4th = 60 s, 5th = DQ, first major 120 s, second = DQ, warning = minor, substitute fills each exclusion, 12 available no forfeit / 11 forfeit, 25 arrows refill, 5 freezes allowed and the 6th refused, new possession resets.  All pass.
- Full games with RULES_V03=1 (4 seeds): no crashes; arrows 21-31 per game and freezes 0-1, so neither archer limit binds in current play.  Minor fouls ~1 per game.
Open: whether 'hold', 'deliberate delay' and 'transition foul' stay minor; the exclusion timer still runs on live time (engine behaviour) - canon says game time, same thing while the clock runs.

## Step 3 (done, first pass): Contact Scrimmage on top of restarts_v1
CORRECTION: the prototype already had restarts_v1.py (First Hunt each quarter, scrimmage restart after a first tackle outside the attacked Keeper Zone, 40 m snap after a score, toss-in; see RESTARTS_NOTES.md).  My earlier statement that the Contact Scrimmage and First Hunt were unbuilt was wrong.  Step 3 therefore changes only the differences from canon.  restarts_v1.py was edited (backup: restarts_v1_before_rv03.py), all edits gated by RULES_V03=1 and RV03_SCRIM (default 1).
Changes: scrimmage also inside the attacked Keeper Zone (no inbound, no 'attacker must touch outside' aperture); first-tackle spot within 10 m of the goal plane resets to 10 m (x only); 10 s snap window (restarts had 6 s); missed window = count +1, spot 5 m back, second = turnover; offence offside = count +1, spot 5 m back; defence offside = count -1 (floor 0), spot 5 m closer to goal (restarts reset it to 0 at the same spot); repeated defensive offside no longer draws a warning/minor (canon: offside is not a minor foul); second tackle = scrimmage for the other team at the tackle spot or its own 40 m, whichever is closer to the goal it attacks; foul awards restart by scrimmage at the ball or foul spot, whichever is closer to the goal attacked; possession clock runs in the snap window only if more than 0:10 remained at the tackle (floored at 0.5 s [P]).
Seen in 4 seeds x 120 s games: 24-28 scrimmages per game, 10 m reset fired 2-4 times, a missed window once, no restart faults, no timeouts.  Offside has not occurred in normal play (the AI waits for legal positions, as in restarts v1), so the offside paths are only exercised by code review so far.
Not done: First Hunt per canon (any player; restarts_v1 picks by role), tackler alignment 'opposite or 5 m back' is restarts_v1's nose / 5 m rule, intent-based major classification, Keeper Zone defender 5 m rule beyond marking_distance.

## Off-ball block option (block_option_v0.py) - findings
Option: a team with block_rate > 0 sends up to round(4 x rate) off-ball attackers to screen the defenders nearest the carrier (BLOCK_MODE naive | predict | persist).  Paired test (30 seeds, sides swapped, 4 comparisons): no detectable effect on points (all 95% ranges include 0).
Why: of assigned blockers only ~4-5% touch their defender (gap <= 0.2 m), ~9% get within 1 m, median closest approach 4-6 m, in all three targeting variants (aim at the defender; aim at his predicted position; persistent assignments to blockers within 9 m).  Defenders are fast and usually already past or well away; screens are assigned for about 1 s.  Note the engine logs a 'block' engagement only when the contact is material, so the count of logged block engagements under-reports gentle contact; the geometric gap above does not.
Suggestion (not built): blocking is a set-piece skill here: at a scrimmage the two lines start about 3 m apart, which is where a screen can actually land.  A 'charge at the snap' option for the line would test that.

## Bounce throws (3 Nov) — bounce_option_v0.py
Option, off by default (genes dribble_rate / skip_rate / bounce_shot_rate = 0; env BOUNCE_OPTION=0 disables). One primitive: throw the ball to a spot on the surface and let the engine's own flight/bounce/roll physics (predict_path) play it.
- Dribble: carrier throws 5–8 m ahead into open space, runs to the landing spot, may replay his own ball once after it has touched the ground (self_recontact waived once per possession spell; spell ends when another player holds the ball). A second dribble is blocked until another player touches it.
- Skip pass: when the straight lane is contested (<2.2 m of a defender) a bounce pass is planned so the post-bounce path passes the receiver's hands; falls back to the normal pass if no plan (common: ~60–85% of flags find no plan).
- Bounce shot: 5–26 m from goal, lands 45–75% of the way and enters the goal plane at 0.25–2.6 m height near the far/open side of the Keeper.
Trial (seeds 3–6, all options at 1.0): dribbles 3–5/game, 1–2 replayed, no self_recontact fouls; bounce shots 3–6/game with no goals confirmed yet. Needs a paired test before drawing conclusions.
